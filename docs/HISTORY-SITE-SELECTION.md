# How the Chernobyl site was chosen, and how the plant was started

Research note, 2026-11-15. Written because the mod's zone currently begins with a
burst of slash commands — `/prombaza pos1`, `/pos2`, `/directorate designate`,
`/zone spawn` — and **nobody in 1970 typed a command into a computer to start a
power station**. The real beginning was a decision, a survey, a helicopter, and a
conversation. This note records what the sources actually say, so the mod can be
rebuilt around people talking, not terminals.

Everything below is tagged **[SOURCED]**, **[SINGLE-SOURCE]** or **[UNVERIFIED]**.

## 1. Why a station was needed at all — the 1966 decree [SOURCED]

The history of ChNPP begins with a **USSR Council of Ministers decree of
29 September 1966** on bringing new generating capacity into service: **11.9 GW
total, of which 8 GW was to be nuclear**. The station was planned for the
**southern part of the Unified Energy System of the USSR**, to supply the
**Central energy region** — 27 oblasts of the Ukrainian SSR plus Rostov oblast, a
population of **53 million**.

Source: ChNPP official site, "Будівництво та експлуатація"
<https://chnpp.gov.ua/ua/about/history-of-the-chnpp-2/chnpp-construction>

## 2. The site survey — 16 points, then 2 finalists [SOURCED]

The selection criterion was **transmission distance**: the most economical radius
to carry the power was about **350–450 km**.

In **1965–1966**, the **Kyiv branch of the design institute «Теплоэлектропроект»**
surveyed **16 candidate construction points** across **Kyiv, Vinnytsia and
Zhytomyr oblasts**.

Two points were put forward as finalists:

- near the village of **Ladyzhyn, Vinnytsia oblast**;
- near the village of **Kopachi, Kyiv oblast** — the one that was chosen.

The station was first called the **«Central Ukrainian NPP»** (Центрально-Українська
АЕС).

Sources: ChNPP official site (as above); ru.wikipedia, "Чернобыльская АЭС"
<https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Чернобыльская_АЭС> (survey of 16 points, Kyiv branch
of Теплоэлектропроект, 1965–1966).

## 3. The decision and the directorate [SOURCED]

By **Order No. 297 of the USSR Minister of Energy and Electrification, of
11 December 1969**, the **Directorate of the NPP under construction** was formed,
located at **Kopachi village, Chernobyl district, Kyiv oblast**, subordinate to
**«Головатомэнерго»**.

Source: ChNPP official site (as above).

The chosen site: **4 km from Kopachi**, on the **right bank of the Pripyat river**,
**15 km from the town of Chernobyl**, next to the **Yanov railway station** on the
Chernigov–Ovruch line. The plant took the name of the *town* (Chernobyl), not the
village it was built beside (Kopachi) — which is why it is the "Chernobyl" plant
next to the city of **Pripyat**.

Source: ru.wikipedia, "Чернобыльская АЭС" (site 4 km from Kopachi, right bank of
the Pripyat, 15 km from Chernobyl, near Yanov station).

## 4. The director — Viktor Bryukhanov [SOURCED]

- Born **1 December 1935, Tashkent**. **[SOURCED — en.wikipedia]**
- 1966: invited to the **Slavyansk Thermal Power Plant**; rose from senior foreman
  to deputy chief engineer. **[SOURCED]**
- **1970**: resigned to go and build the nuclear plant in the Ukrainian SSR.
  **Director of ChNPP 1970–1986.** **[SOURCED]**
- **1971**: the energy minister offered him the assignment — a plant of **four
  RBMK reactors on the Pripyat**. He **initially proposed PWR (VVER) reactors**
  but was overruled on safety-and-economic grounds in favour of the RBMK.
  **[SOURCED — single source: Higginbotham, *Midnight in Chernobyl*]**

Source: en.wikipedia, "Viktor Bryukhanov"
<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Bryukhanov>, citing Higginbotham.

## 5. The first concrete, and the first unit [SOURCED]

- **15 August 1972**: the **first cubic metre of concrete** laid in the foundation
  of the main building. (The infobox date "construction began 15 August 1972"
  matches.)
- **26 September 1977**: Unit 1 commissioned (turbogenerator No. 2 synchronised to
  the grid) — the first nuclear plant on Ukrainian soil.

Source: ChNPP official site; en.wikipedia, "Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant"
<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernobyl_Nuclear_Power_Plant>.

## 6. Pripyat and the construction base [SOURCED]

- **Pripyat founded 4 February 1970**; proclaimed a city in **1979**; population
  ~49,360 before the 1986 evacuation. **[SOURCED — en.wikipedia, "Pripyat"]**
- The works were an "all-union" effort: **over 20 specialised organisations**
  (electrical, heat insulation, hydraulic engineering, chemical protection …) on
  site at once, coordinated by a **headquarters** under site manager **Vasilii
  Trofimovich Kizima**. A **mobile crane lifting prefabricated units up to 640 t**
  was used — the only one of its kind in the country. The reactor shaft was
  carcassed by the Komsomol-youth brigade of **Viacheslav Volkov** by **1974**.
  **[SOURCED — en.wikipedia, citing Ukrtelefilm 1974]**
- Construction was beset by **material shortages and inferior materials, labour
  shortages, and falling behind schedule**; as it fell behind, **building safety
  was neglected**. **[SOURCED — Klüppelberg, "Creating Chernobyl", NTM 2025,
  doi:10.1007/s00048-025-00424-6, cited by en.wikipedia]**

## 8. What this means for the mod

The real sequence was:

1. a **decree** sets the need (not a player action);
2. a **survey** produces candidates (**16 points, 2 finalists**);
3. a **decision** picks one (Ladyzhyn vs Kopachi) — made by **people in a room**;
4. a **directorate is formed** at the chosen village;
5. a **director arrives** and **people begin work** (tents, mud, the first
   concrete two years later).

There is **no terminal** anywhere in that. The player's job is the job of the
**director**: to be told the candidates, to choose, to arrive, and to talk to the
people who do the work. See the design note in
`docs/SITE-SELECTION-DESIGN.md`.
